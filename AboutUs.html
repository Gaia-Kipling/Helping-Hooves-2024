Helping Hooves is a non-profit organization dedicated to the welfare of unwanted and abused equines, ranging from mules to zebras. Our mission is to provide a safe haven for these animals and educate the community on compassionate animal care.

We believe all equines deserve respect and strive to inform rather than confiscate, offering various services including rescue, adoption, surrender, and community outreach.
