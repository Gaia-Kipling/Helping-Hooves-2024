Our Equine Rescue Services

Helping Hooves provides immediate response and support for equine rescues. Our team evaluates reported cases of abuse, neglect, and emergency situations involving horses, donkeys, and other equines. If a rescue is required, we will confiscate the animal from the owner and take legal action if necessary to ensure their well-being.

If you know of an animal in need, please fill out the form or contact us for assistance.
